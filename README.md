# Anscombe's *Intention*: a layered companion

A private study companion to G. E. M. Anscombe, *Intention* (Blackwell 1957; 2nd ed. 1963; Harvard University Press 2000). It aims to give an **objective account** of what the book says, section by section, at increasing levels of detail. It also gathers and evaluates what commentators have said about it. It is meant to be read alongside the book, not instead of it, and it deliberately leaves out any interpretive agenda of its own. Where an editorial judgment is made, it is labelled "*Editorial note*".

## How to use the levels

The summary is built for **progressive disclosure**. Start at the top and drill down wherever you want more.

| Level | File(s) | Grain | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| **L0** | [summary/L0-overview.md](summary/L0-overview.md) | the whole book | one paragraph + about one page |
| **L1** | [summary/L1-parts.md](summary/L1-parts.md) | 7 parts (movements of the argument) | a few paragraphs each |
| **L2** | [summary/L2-sections/](summary/L2-sections/) | 17 section groups | the argument step by step, with examples and connections |
| **L3** | [summary/L3-sections/](summary/L3-sections/) | each of §§1–52 | a dense précis, main claim, examples, objections, cross-references, commentators |

Every file has a breadcrumb line linking **up** (to the overview, its part, its group) and **across** (previous and next). Every higher-level file links **down** to the level below.

Cross-cutting files:
- [glossary.md](glossary.md): Anscombe's technical terms, with the § where each is introduced.
- [debates.md](debates.md): the main interpretive disputes, set out neutrally with who holds what.
- [reading-paths.md](reading-paths.md): first pass, second pass, and deep-dive routes.

## Conventions

- **§ references** are to Anscombe's numbered sections. Pages, where given, are those of the 2nd edition (1963), which the Harvard 2000 paperback reproduces. They come from Anscombe's analytic table of contents. Pagination in the 1957/58 printings diverges from p. 59 onward; see [Sandis](sources/secondary/sandis-2015-klesis-anscombes-intentions.md).
- **Anscombe vs commentators.** Statements of what the text says carry § references. Statements of what a commentator says cite the commentator's file in [`sources/secondary/`](sources/secondary/). The two are kept in separate subsections of each L3 and L2 file.
- **† marker.** In L2 and L3 files, "†" marks a detail drawn from the writer's recollection of Anscombe's text that could not be corroborated from her contents line or a saved secondary source. Check those against the book.
- **(reported)** in [debates.md](debates.md) means a view known only through another commentator's report (see [UNAVAILABLE](sources/UNAVAILABLE.md)).

## Sources

- [`sources/secondary/`](sources/secondary/): 30 secondary accounts. Each has front matter giving author, citation/URL, access date, type, §§ covered and interpretive slant, followed by a faithful paraphrase of the account.
- [`sources/EVALUATION.md`](sources/EVALUATION.md): every source assessed for accuracy against Anscombe's contents, coverage, slant, reliability and depth. Includes a ranking, a list of known mis-citations, and a table of which source to trust for what.
- [`sources/UNAVAILABLE.md`](sources/UNAVAILABLE.md): sources sought but not accessible, with whatever is known about them.

## Fair use

This repository is private and intended only for personal study. It does **not** contain the text of *Intention* or substantial extracts from it. Quotations from Anscombe are limited to short terminological phrases with § references, such as "Why?", "known without observation", "practical knowledge", "desirability characterisation" and "cause of what it understands". Secondary sources are paraphrased, with brief attributed quotations only. Section boundaries and topics were checked against the book's analytic table of contents. The full text was not reproduced. Commentators' work belongs to its authors; follow the citations to read them properly.
