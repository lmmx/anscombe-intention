# Reading paths

[README](README.md) · [Overview](summary/L0-overview.md) · [Parts](summary/L1-parts.md) · [Glossary](glossary.md) · [Debates](debates.md)

Three ways through the book and this repo, with notes on where readers usually get stuck. Each path assumes you have the book open; the repo is a companion to it, not a substitute.

## 1. First pass: get the shape (about a weekend)

1. Read [L0](summary/L0-overview.md), then the "Why these boundaries" table and Part I of [L1](summary/L1-parts.md).
2. Read §§1–8 of the book straight through. After each section, check yourself against its L3 file ([§01](summary/L3-sections/§01.md) … [§08](summary/L3-sections/§08.md)). Fix three things in your mind: the three heads (§1), the "Why?" question (§5), and the refusals and knowledge without observation (§§6–8).
3. Skim §§9–15 (mental causes and motives) using only [G04](summary/L2-sections/G04-s09-11-mental-causality.md) and [G05](summary/L2-sections/G05-s12-15-motives.md). Then read §§16–18 properly ([G06](summary/L2-sections/G06-s16-18-the-range-of-answers.md)), since §18 fixes the definition.
4. Read §§23–26 (the pump and the A–D order) carefully ([G08](summary/L2-sections/G08-s22-27-intention-with-which-and-the-a-d-order.md)).
5. Read §§28–32 ([G09](summary/L2-sections/G09-s28-30-knowledge-of-ones-own-action.md), [G10](summary/L2-sections/G10-s31-32-orders-and-the-shopping-list.md)), ending on the shopping list.
6. Jump to §§45–48 ([G14](summary/L2-sections/G14-s44-45-practical-knowledge-and-failure.md), [G15](summary/L2-sections/G15-s46-48-form-of-description-and-practical-knowledge.md)) and read them twice.
7. Read §§50–52 ([G17](summary/L2-sections/G17-s50-52-intention-for-the-future-revisited.md)) to see the circle close.
8. Leave §§33–43 for the second pass.

Secondary reading on this pass: [Setiya, SEP "Intention"](sources/secondary/setiya-2022-sep-intention.md) and [Thompson, REP](sources/secondary/thompson-1998-rep-anscombe.md).

*Common stumbling points:*
- §§9–15 look like a digression. Their job is to fence off the special sense of "Why?" from evidence, mental causes and motives.
- §20 is compressed.
- In §§23–26, keep track of *one* action with *several* descriptions.

## 3. Deep dive: by problem (open-ended)

Pick a thread and follow it across the book, using L3 files and the source files they cite.

- **The epistemology of agency.** §§6–8 → §28 → §§29–31 → §32 → §45 → §48 → §52. Debates [§1](debates.md#1-knowledge-without-observation-what-is-known-and-how-central-is-it) and [§2](debates.md#2-practical-knowledge-as-the-cause-of-what-it-understands-48-and-failure-45). Read [Özaltun](sources/secondary/ozaltun-2016-practical-knowledge-section-45.md), [Setiya 2016](sources/secondary/setiya-2016-anscombe-practical-knowledge.md), [Small](sources/secondary/small-2020-action-practical-knowledge.md) and [Campbell 2022](sources/secondary/campbell-2022-practical-knowledge-practical-truth.md) against each other. Then the unavailable core: Thompson, *Life and Action*; Moran & Stone; Haddock; Rödl ([UNAVAILABLE](sources/UNAVAILABLE.md)).
- **Reasons, causes and Davidson.** §§5, 9–16, 19, 23–26 → [debates §3](debates.md#3-anscombe-and-davidson-causal-theories-of-action). Read [Speaks](sources/secondary/speaks-2009-guide-1-31.md) (causalist-friendly) against [Frey](sources/secondary/frey-2016-action-practical-knowledge-good.md) (anti-causalist) and [Schwenkler](sources/secondary/schwenkler-2019-guide.md) (neither). Then Davidson's "Actions, Reasons, and Causes" and "Intending".
- **The form of practical reasoning.** §§33–44, with §42 tied back to §26. [Debates §8](debates.md#8-practical-reasoning-calculation-the-good-and-form-3343). Read [Sandis](sources/secondary/sandis-2015-klesis-anscombes-intentions.md) on the 1963 §33, [Elliott](sources/secondary/elliott-2016-anscombe-practical-truth.md), [Haddock & Wiseman](sources/secondary/haddock-wiseman-2022-anscombean-mind.md) (Rödl, Newton and Small on Kant). Then Anscombe's later "Practical Inference".
- **Unity of the three heads.** §1 → §§2–4 → §§20–22 → §§50–52. [Debates §5](debates.md#5-intention-for-the-future-and-the-unity-of-the-three-heads). Read [Wiseman](sources/secondary/wiseman-2016-guidebook.md), [Millgram](sources/secondary/millgram-2026-intention-of-intention.md), [Setiya SEP](sources/secondary/setiya-2022-sep-intention.md), and Moran & Stone (if accessible).
- **Intention, foresight and ethics.** §§22, 25 and 49 → [debates §7](debates.md#7-intention-versus-foresight-double-effect) and [§9](debates.md#9-what-is-the-book-for). Then Anscombe's "Mr Truman's Degree", "Modern Moral Philosophy" and "War and Murder".
- **Edition and text.** [Sandis](sources/secondary/sandis-2015-klesis-anscombes-intentions.md) for the variants in §§2, 6, 17, 19 and 33–34.

*Editorial note:* the suggested orders reflect where the secondary literature concentrates its attention. They are not a claim about which sections matter most to Anscombe.
